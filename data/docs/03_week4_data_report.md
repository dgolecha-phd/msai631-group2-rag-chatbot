# Assignment: Week 4 Data Report
The Week 4 data report is worth 25 percent of the course grade. It must be two to three pages, double spaced, in APA format.
Choose one public dataset from the approved list in the Week 4 module. Describe where the data came from, report the mean and median of one numeric column, and explain in plain language what the numbers show and what they do not show.
The report needs a title page, at least three references, and one table or chart with a caption. A report that only lists numbers without interpretation will lose points under the clarity row of the rubric.
Submit the report as a Word document or PDF through the Week 4 assignment link. Do not email the report.
