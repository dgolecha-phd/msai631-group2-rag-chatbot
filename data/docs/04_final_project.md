# Assignment: Final Project
The final project is worth 35 percent of the course grade and is due on Sunday of Week 8 at 11:59 p.m. Central Time.
Students pick a question they care about, find one dataset that could answer it, and write a four to five page analysis. The analysis must state the question, describe the data, show one chart, explain one limitation, and give a recommendation.
A proposal of one paragraph is due in Week 6. The instructor gives feedback within three days. Students must work alone: no partners and no groups. Group submissions are not accepted.
The rubric rows are question quality, data description, chart quality, limitations, recommendation, and writing clarity.
