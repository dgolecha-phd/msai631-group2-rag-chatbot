# Study Tips: Preparing for Quizzes and the Final Project
Quizzes cover only the reading for that week. Reread the learning objectives at the top of the reading and make sure you can explain each one in your own words.
Test yourself instead of rereading. Close the notes and write down the key terms from memory, then check what you missed. Explaining a concept out loud to another person also shows quickly which parts you do not understand.
For the final project, write the question and the recommendation first. Then work backward to the chart and the data that support them.
