# Study Tips: Managing Your Time in an Online Course
Block fixed times each week for the course and treat them like class meetings. Two sessions of 90 minutes work better for most students than one long session on Sunday night.
Start each week by reading the module overview and listing every due date. Do the reading first, the quiz second, and the discussion post last, because the discussion is easier after the quiz has tested your understanding.
Submit assignments at least a few hours before the deadline. Slow uploads and login problems are the most common reasons for late work.
