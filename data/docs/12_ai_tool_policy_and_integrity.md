# Academic Integrity and Use of AI Tools
Students may use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini to brainstorm ideas, check grammar, and understand a concept. Students may not submit text written by an AI tool as their own work.
If you use an AI tool in any way on an assignment, add a short note at the end that names the tool and says what you used it for. Assignments without a note that are later found to contain AI-written text are treated as an academic integrity violation.
Always verify facts from an AI tool against a reliable source, because AI tools can state false information with confidence. Copying work from another student or from the internet without citation is plagiarism and is also an integrity violation.
