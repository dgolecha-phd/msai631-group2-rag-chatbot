# Concept: Mean and Median
The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value when the values are sorted. If there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle ones.
The mean is pulled toward extreme values. The median is not. For incomes, a few very high earners raise the mean far above what a typical person earns, so the median is usually a better description of a typical case.
When the mean and median are far apart, the data is skewed. Reporting both tells the reader more than reporting either one alone.
