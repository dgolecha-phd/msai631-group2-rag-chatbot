# Concept: Sampling and Sampling Bias
A population is the whole group you want to learn about. A sample is the smaller group you actually collect data from. A good sample represents the population.
Sampling bias happens when some members of the population are more likely to be included than others. An online survey about internet habits, for example, leaves out people who rarely go online.
A random sample gives every member the same chance of selection and reduces bias. A larger sample reduces random error but does not fix bias. Always state how a sample was chosen when you describe a dataset.
