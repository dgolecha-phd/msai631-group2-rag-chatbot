# Technical Support
If you cannot log in, first reset your password through the university login page. If that does not work, contact the university help desk by email or phone. The help desk is open on weekdays and has limited weekend hours.
If an upload fails, try a different browser, turn off browser extensions, and check that the file is under 25 MB. Save a screenshot of any error message.
Technical problems close to a deadline should be reported to the help desk and to the instructor right away. Keep the help desk ticket number as proof of the problem.
