# Course Overview: SAMPLE 101 Introduction to Data Literacy
SAMPLE 101 is an eight-week online course that teaches students how to read, question, and explain data. It has no prerequisites. Each week has one reading, one short quiz, and one discussion post.
The course grade has four parts, which are the grading parts of the course: weekly discussion posts (20%), weekly quizzes (20%), the Week 4 data report (25%), and the final project (35%).
All course materials are posted in the learning management system under the Modules tab. Each week opens on Monday at 12:00 a.m. Central Time and closes the following Sunday at 11:59 p.m. Central Time.
