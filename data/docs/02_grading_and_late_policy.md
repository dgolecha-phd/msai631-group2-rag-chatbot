# Grading Scale and Late Work Policy
The grading scale is: A = 90 to 100, B = 80 to 89, C = 70 to 79, D = 60 to 69, and F = below 60.
Late work loses 10 percent of the available points for each day it is late. Work more than three days late is not accepted unless the instructor approved an extension before the due date.
Extensions are granted for documented emergencies such as illness or a family crisis. Send a message to the instructor before the deadline and attach any documentation. Discussion posts cannot be made up after the week closes.
Quizzes may be taken once. The quiz closes on Sunday at 11:59 p.m. Central Time and cannot be reopened.
