# Instructor Contact and Office Hours
Office hours are held online on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time. Students join through the link in the Course Information module. No appointment is needed.
For questions outside office hours, send a message through the learning management system inbox. The instructor replies within 48 hours on weekdays. Messages sent on Friday evening or over the weekend are answered on Monday.
For urgent technical problems on a due date, contact the help desk first and then tell the instructor what happened.
